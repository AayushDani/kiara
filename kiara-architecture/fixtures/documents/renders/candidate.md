## How to submit a privacy request

Clause: a84b9e93-a656-5e98-9d5a-d7be19e46954

To request access, deletion or correction, email privacy@democo.example or visit https://democo.example/privacy/requests. Tell us your request type and the email associated with your account; please do not send your password. You may submit a request without creating an account. We will acknowledge a request to know, delete or correct within 10 business days and ordinarily respond within 45 calendar days after receipt. If reasonably necessary, we may extend that period by up to 45 additional days and explain the reason within the first 45 days. If we cannot fulfill a request in whole or in part, we will explain the applicable basis and respond as the law requires.

## How we verify requests

Clause: 3618409a-546d-57ed-976d-10114cd85254

For an existing account, we generally ask you to authenticate using the account and may ask you to confirm the request through the account email. For a person who cannot access an account, we compare information you provide with records we already hold. We request only information reasonably necessary for verification in light of the request and information involved. We do not require you to create an account. If we cannot verify a request, we explain this and any available alternatives. We use information collected for verification only for verification, security or fraud prevention. We delete any new personal information collected for verification as soon as practical after processing your request, except records required by the applicable privacy-request recordkeeping rules.

## Authorized agents
