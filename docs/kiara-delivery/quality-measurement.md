# Attributed rework and repeated issues

The Activity effort comparison now shows correction minutes and completed correction intervals as a subset of all recorded human time. It does not add that subset to the total again. Voided entries and running timers are excluded from the correction sum. The comparable baseline and resulting time difference remain separate, with estimates labeled and no automatic financial-savings claim.

Activity also accepts effort and an attributed comparable baseline for a private value goal before any matter exists. Goal-only setup, review and correction are shown in a separate total; they are never silently added to a later matter total. A goal-only difference is withheld until the person reports that goal achieved with a still-available useful-output receipt, no timer is running, and the workspace is not a fictional rehearsal. The displayed difference is limited to the selected goal's recorded work and is not validated savings or a total across goals and matters. If one outcome spans a goal and a later matter, their separate comparisons must be reconciled before making any combined effort claim.

Feedback on an exact retained answer, draft or proposal can include an optional reviewer-chosen issue key. The same key on two distinct outputs in the same audience and feedback category appears as a reported repeat issue. Withdrawn reports, inaccessible outputs, different audiences and different categories do not combine. Kiara does not infer that two free-text complaints describe the same defect. These counts describe attributed reports, not an independently adjudicated error rate.

The frozen strategy evaluation and shadow path remains the gate for the two supported executable behavior changes. An issue key cannot promote a fact, legal decision, strategy or action. Customer quality baselines and qualified domain adjudication require external review before the reports can support a product-quality or savings claim.

The read-only paired-case qualification command requires the observed baseline to be bound, when recorded, to the exact completed comparison matter and its full attributed effort. Activity can still show external observed baselines and older free-text baselines, but those records do not satisfy this paired-case gate. A linked baseline requires equal matter and effort audiences; its case identity and amount disappear from a viewer who loses access to the comparison matter or its effort. A changed comparison case is marked stale and its numeric difference is withheld until a successor baseline is reviewed. The gate also checks that the named independent evaluator has a current membership that can read both compared matters. These remain structural checks; real comparability, the adjudication artifact and customer-observed outcomes still need independent authentication.
