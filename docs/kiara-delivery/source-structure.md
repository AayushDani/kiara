# Structure-aware source evidence

Implemented in `src/v2/source-structure.ts`, consumed by both local retrieval and Atlas chunk hydration. Quotes remain exact slices of retained text, with UTF-16 character offsets, detected structure, parent anchors and the parser version. Heuristics identify Markdown/named section headings, numbered clauses, quoted definitions, pipe/tab tables and schedules. Detected references, definitions and exception context are separate cited excerpts; they do not imply a legal interpretation or prove applicability.

Context expansion is bounded to eight chunks per selected record. Final conversation evidence is bounded to 24 excerpts and 40,000 characters. A limitation reports detected context omitted by the bound. Agreement inventory enumeration remains independent of excerpt ranking and lists every eligible executed agreement head within its own explicit bound. A complete notice conclusion still requires inventory attestation and qualified agreement-specific review.

`clauses-definitions-context-v1` is part of each immutable hybrid chunk's identity. Existing character-window index rows are ineligible for the new exact allowlist; an operator must explicitly synchronize the selected current records. Index setup does not silently spend money or rebuild inside a customer question. Search itself still uses exact/keyword/vector branches with reciprocal rank fusion; there is no learned reranker in this checkpoint.

Provider chronology eligibility is checked before local excerpts or indexed IDs are selected and again before model dispatch/output admission. Historical source observations remain readable for repair under their ordinary ACL, but cannot supply current evidence. Older conversation turns with non-current lineage are omitted from a new prompt; a current user message with stale lineage fails visibly. Coverage can still report stale/unsupported scope without offering stale source text as current evidence.

Qualification: `evidence/source-structure-tests.log` records **39/39** focused parser, retrieval, hybrid and AI tests. These cover exact anchors and Unicode, definitions/exception/schedule context, cyclic references and truncation, six-agreement enumeration, stale index/access gates, injection, budgets and durable model replay using injected providers. `evidence/chronology-counsel-coverage.log` records **26/26** counsel, legal maintenance and learning/coverage tests after current-lineage checks. These are software tests on synthetic examples, not empirical legal-answer quality, a broad contract-parsing benchmark, live Atlas Search or paid-model qualification. Independent reviewer evidence is recorded separately.
