# U.S. federal legal-reference source operations

## Initial source and scope decision

Use the Government Publishing Office's **GovInfo** API to inspect one selected U.S. Code or annual Code of Federal Regulations granule. GPO describes GovInfo as free access to official federal publications and documents a free `api.data.gov` key, package/granule metadata, version identifiers and content links. The implementation uses `X-Api-Key` in a server-side header, not a URL or browser request. GPO's own API README states current default limits of 36,000/hour, 1,200/minute and 40/second; response headers remain the operational source of truth. Kiara should budget much less: at most 100 selected URLs per tenant under its legal-source policy, daily checks at most, and no open-ended search or corpus import. GovInfo source-access fees are $0 according to GPO's free-access and free-key descriptions; encrypted storage, worker, review labor and optional embeddings have separate costs and require their own spending policy. [GovInfo developer hub](https://www.govinfo.gov/developers), [official API README](https://github.com/usgpo/api), [API key guidance](https://api.data.gov/docs/developer-manual/), [GovInfo free access](https://www.govinfo.gov/about).

`src/v2/legal-reference-govinfo.ts` limits admission to an exact selected `USCODE` or annual `CFR` package/granule. It checks the API metadata identity, the selected HTML (U.S. Code) or XML (annual CFR) rendition and PDF format, URL host/path, content bounds and the tenant's exact URL read policy. It stages a legal `Source` and `LegalAuthority` with metadata, source hash and encrypted original source bytes. The authority begins **unverified**, with no effective date and no coverage entry. The selected text is a research aid; the linked official PDF, later amendments, currentness and applicability must be inspected by a qualified reviewer. U.S. Code editions and annual CFR editions are not proof of today's complete law. The U.S. Code includes titles that are only prima facie evidence; for those titles the Statutes at Large govern. [GPO U.S. Code guidance](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/uscode), [GPO CFR guidance](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/cfr).

The eCFR and FederalRegister.gov APIs may help discover changes, but their sites describe their web renditions as informational or unofficial for legal reliance. A reviewer must verify relevant changes against current official CFR, Federal Register and affected-section materials on GovInfo before updating a legal conclusion. No state, local, EU, international, case-law, industry-specialist or exhaustive U.S. federal coverage is inferred from this initial feed. [eCFR legal status](https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/understanding-the-ecfr/what-is-the-ecfr), [Federal Register API and legal status](https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1).

## Multi-source legal context

GovInfo is one connector, not the legal corpus or a default answer source for every matter. Kiara should first determine the issue, jurisdiction, governing dates and source type, then search only sources whose coverage and reuse terms fit that question. A privacy question, for example, can require federal and state statutes, an agency's current regulations and guidance, applicable decisions, and the customer's own policies or contracts. [California's privacy regulator publishes its current CCPA law and regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/); [EUR-Lex provides EU legal documents and machine-readable retrieval](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/help/data-reuse/webservice.html).

| Source role | Examples and use | Admission rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controlling public text | GovInfo for selected U.S. federal editions; the relevant state regulator or legislature; EUR-Lex for EU law | Capture exact version, jurisdiction, effective date, original bytes and official link; qualified review decides applicability. |
| Discovery and change signal | eCFR, Federal Register, [Open States state-legislation API](https://docs.openstates.org/api-v3/) | Lead a reviewer to a controlling source; a bill, proposal, alert or aggregator record does not itself establish current law. |
| Case law and citator | [CourtListener's case-law and citation APIs](https://wiki.free.law/c/courtlistener/help/api/rest/v4/rest-api-v47), or a licensed commercial research service | Check court, opinion status, date and later treatment; confirm commercial API/reuse rights before integration. |
| Secondary analysis | Treatises, articles, AI research services and licensed provider commentary | Label as interpretation, retain attribution and license scope, and trace any legal assertion to reviewed primary material. |
| Customer-specific rules | Signed contracts, approved policies and playbooks | Keep tenant permissions and provenance; distinguish private obligations from public law. |

The shared `LegalAuthority` and `CoverageEntry` records can represent more than GovInfo, but this release has no live connector or coverage qualification for the other rows. A later connector should return a common candidate envelope with provider, source class, jurisdiction, stable citation, version/effective dates, retrieved-at time, original hash, rights terms and review status. Retrieval should exclude unverified legal candidates from answer authority. AI can rank leads and compare versions; it cannot upgrade a candidate into applicable law without source and reviewer evidence. Commercial APIs such as [vLex's research API](https://developer.vlex.com/apis) are possible product choices after pricing, coverage, licensing and data-use review.

A member can also supply an exact source manually and register it with `coverage.source.add` for a declared jurisdiction and domain. Registration now gates that supplied source out of answer retrieval until its current version is verified by a legal reviewer and the matching coverage scope receives a separate review. This supports a bounded non-GovInfo review path without implying that Kiara has fetched, licensed, checked for later treatment, or qualified any third-party database.

For a no-key title-level freshness signal, an operator can run `node --import tsx scripts/v2-ecfr-currentness.ts --title N`. The bounded reader makes one read-only request to the [official eCFR title catalog](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/titles.json) and reports its catalog date, title amendment/issue dates, up-to-date-as-of date, and import status as `unreviewed_discovery`. It makes no tenant or Mongo write and is not a section-level monitor, a substitute for official-source verification, or evidence of applicability. A [connected Title 16 read](evidence/ecfr-title-currentness-sep27.md) confirmed the API shape on 27 September 2026; operational rate policy and ongoing availability were not qualified.

For a no-key lead list on an exact CFR title and part, an operator can run `node --import tsx scripts/v2-federal-register-discovery.ts --title N --part N`. The reader makes one bounded request to the [public Federal Register API](https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1), shows at most ten newest indexed documents, their types and official GovInfo PDF links, and reports the total count and whether more results exist. The site states that its API needs no key and that its web rendition is unofficial for legal reliance. A proposed rule is explicitly labelled; the command never treats a result as enacted, complete or applicable. The output remains `unreviewed_discovery`, makes no tenant or Mongo write, and requires qualified review of the official PDF, affected sections, effective dates and later changes. A [connected sample read](evidence/federal-register-discovery-sep27.md) confirmed the API shape for 16 CFR part 1235 on 27 September 2026. Indexing, availability and legal completeness across other CFR selections remain unqualified.

Discovery rejects returned document links whose publication date or document number differs from the metadata, or whose URL contains credentials or a nonstandard port. A valid link is still a discovery lead, not a verified legal source.

CourtListener is a possible later source for case-law discovery and citation checks, subject to an explicit product and rights decision. Its [current membership API policy](https://free.law/membership/allowed-api-usage/) bars powering a revenue-positive commercial product or internal organizational tool under membership access and directs commercial users to arrange terms with Free Law Project. The [default authenticated quota](https://wiki.free.law/c/courtlistener/help/api/rest/v4/rest-api-v47) is 5 requests/minute, 50/hour and 125/day. The low-cost individual membership prices are therefore not a production license for Kiara. No CourtListener connector, account or commercial agreement is present in this release candidate.

GPO states that U.S. government works are generally in the public domain, while third-party material included in government publications may retain copyright. The legal-content owner must examine selected material and attribution before republication, embedding or customer display of lengthy excerpts. The system retains selected originals for verification; it does not treat an agency publication as a blanket license for third-party material. [GPO copyright and reuse policy](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies).

## Operator and reviewer sequence

1. A content owner chooses exact package/granule IDs and the narrow domain. Configure `KIARA_GOVINFO_API_KEY` privately and a tenant `KIARA_V2_LEGAL_SOURCE_POLICY` with the exact resulting `https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/.../html/...htm` URL for U.S. Code or `/xml/...xml` URL for annual CFR, expiry and byte bound. Do not use `DEMO_KEY` for deployment. No policy means no read.
2. A signed-in legal reviewer uses `POST /api/v2/legal/govinfo` with `{ "operation": "preview", "packageId", "granuleId", "domain" }` and the session CSRF token. The protected route returns the full extracted text, metadata, official PDF link, raw hash and preview hash without writing a source. After inspecting that exact result, the reviewer posts the same selection with `operation: "stage"` and `expectedPreviewHash`. The server re-reads and rejects changed metadata or bytes before retaining the encrypted raw original and creating an unverified authority. The preview hash binds bytes and metadata; it does not prove a human read or legal judgment. The route uses the authenticated session's tenant and actor; the body cannot claim either. It does not auto-create coverage, embed the source or accept a legal conclusion. No general customer-facing selection control is present in this checkpoint.
3. The reviewer compares the exact staged source, hash, metadata, official PDF, later amendments and relevant jurisdiction/domain; checks any third-party content rights; records `coverage.source.verify` with the source version, evidence and review due date. A business owner defines the bounded coverage set and limitations; a qualified reviewer records `coverage.review`. Only a current source plus available coverage enters ordinary answer and Atlas retrieval.

   A registered authority for a URL-backed source must use that retained source's exact HTTPS URL. Registration, reviewer verification and later currentness checks enforce the match; existing mismatches make coverage stale. A manually supplied source with no retained URL can still carry a reviewer-declared reference link, but that link is not independently verified as the source's origin.
4. The reviewer configures an exact legal-source watch under the same policy. A changed page retains new original bytes, expires current freshness and creates an unassessed change. The reviewer inspects old/new hashes, assesses applicability to named work, optionally adopts the source, and repeats source and coverage review. An unassessed change does not itself reopen every matter or update playbooks. See [legal maintenance](legal-maintenance.md).
5. If the source is unavailable, stale, outside declared coverage or the reviewer is unavailable, mark coverage unsupported or pending, keep the legal task owned, and use the existing exportable counsel packet. Existing counsel requires named identity, conflicts evidence, exact terms/fee cap and explicit sharing; the referral route remains unavailable until qualified provider capacity is established. No API supplies qualified advice or counsel engagement.

For a budget illustration, 100 selected pages at one check per day produce at most about 3,000 page reads in 30 days; a new selection adds one metadata request. This is an operational cap, not a throughput target or a storage/embedding cost quote. The selected URL byte limit caps a single response, and indexing requires a separate tenant policy and spend control. The content lead owns quarterly scope/rights review, the qualified reviewer owns source freshness and applicability, and product/legal operations own counsel-provider availability and customer-facing claims.

## Qualification status

The focused `tests/v2-legal-reference-govinfo.test.ts` uses fictional metadata and injected HTTP readers. It verifies exact metadata identity, official format links, policy denial before source reads, encrypted original retention, no legal answer before review, qualified local test gating and stale recheck. The initial watch check compares fetched bytes with the retained original's raw SHA-256, so identical content does not create a false legal change simply because extracted text has a different hash. The earlier GovInfo and legal-maintenance tests passed **7/7** ([log](evidence/legal-watch-baseline-sep27.log)); after adding annual CFR XML, the focused local suite passed **8/8** and the assembled suite passed **615/615** ([log](evidence/govinfo-cfr-full-tests-sep27.log)). These tests establish local code behavior only. Later [read-only public GovInfo samples](evidence/govinfo-public-read-sep27.md) confirmed actual U.S. Code and annual CFR metadata shapes against Kiara's parser and official HTML/XML reads against Kiara's bounded reader. They used `DEMO_KEY` only for public summaries. On 28 September, the owner supplied a private key, and the [selected GovInfo/Atlas check](evidence/govinfo-private-key-atlas-sep28.md) passed exact preview/stage, encrypted-original readback and generated-database cleanup; the Render worker then received the corrected key and returned metadata HTTP 200 for that granule. No named qualified U.S. legal reviewer has verified the source. The hosted reviewer route, sustained reads, source rights, current-law coverage and counsel/domain adjudication remain unqualified. Do not label the release as legal-source live qualified until those checks and human evidence are recorded.
