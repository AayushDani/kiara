# Test candidate privacy notice · working draft

Status: synthetic, unpublished draft for counsel and business review. Version: 1, 2026-09-28. This is not a live privacy notice or a statement about Dice.

## Who and what

Test would operate a US technology hiring marketplace. In the proposed flow, Test collects account identifiers, contact details, skills, work history supplied by the candidate, role preferences, applications, recruiter communications, device/security events, and privacy choices. Sensitive information and resume attachments require a separate data inventory before this notice can be published.

## Why and to whom

Proposed purposes are account operation, job search, candidate-requested applications, candidate-authorized profile discovery, matching suggestions, service support, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Proposed recipients are the employers selected by an application, eligible employer users when a candidate enables discoverability, and contracted service providers with limited purposes. The exact sale/share/targeted-advertising analysis, provider list, and whether employers independently retain copies are unresolved.

## Visibility and choice

The pilot design keeps a profile private until a candidate chooses employer visibility. Applying to a job shares that application with that employer. Changing profile visibility would stop future discovery in Test; it cannot retract an application or a copy already received by an employer. The UI and notices must show that difference before launch.

## Matching and decisions

Test Match would suggest roles and eligible profiles from job criteria and candidate-supplied information. The pilot design does not automatically reject or hire anyone. Before any real launch, Test must document model inputs, human involvement, accuracy and bias tests, accommodation and contest routes, applicable notices, and whether a local automated employment decision tool rule applies.

## Rights, retention, and contact

Candidate rights and response steps depend on jurisdiction and verified identity. The request process must include access, correction, deletion and opt-out routes where applicable, with auditable exceptions and downstream deletion. Retention periods, legal hold criteria, contact address, effective date, corporate identity, and state-specific addenda are **TBD**. Do not publish or rely on this draft until completed and reviewed.
