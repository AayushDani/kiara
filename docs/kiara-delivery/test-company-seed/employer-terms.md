# Test employer subscription and posting terms · working draft

Status: synthetic, unsigned draft. Version: 1, 2026-09-28. No customer relationship, invoice, or signed order is represented.

## Order and access

An employer order would identify the legal customer, authorized recruiter seats, subscription term, fees, products, and permitted business use. Test would provision role-limited access after identity and account checks. The employer is responsible for its users, job content, lawful hiring criteria, and the people it contacts.

## Candidate information

Employer access to visible profiles and applications would be solely for genuine recruitment and the specific authorized hiring purpose. The employer must not scrape, resell, transfer to unrelated parties, use for unrelated marketing, or retain candidate data beyond a documented lawful need. A candidate's choice to share through Test does not waive employer obligations under privacy, employment, anti-discrimination, or background-screening law.

## Job content and hiring decisions

The employer must post genuine technology roles with accurate location, work arrangement, compensation information where required, and a valid application route. The employer makes all interview, screening, hiring, and employment decisions. Test may moderate suspicious or prohibited posts and pause access while reviewing credible misuse. If the employer uses external background reports or automated decision tools, it must assess the applicable obligations; the agreement must allocate Test's own obligations separately.

## Security and incident cooperation

Each party would protect credentials and report suspected unauthorized use promptly through designated contacts. The parties would coordinate candidate requests and security incidents affecting shared data under a signed data-processing addendum where appropriate.

## Open commercial and legal terms

Fees, service levels, warranties, liability cap, indemnities, audit rights, retention, deletion, governing law, notice addresses, order form, effective date, and signature blocks are **TBD**. No clause here is an executed commitment.
