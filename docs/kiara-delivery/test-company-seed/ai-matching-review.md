# Test Match · launch assessment and decision record

Status: synthetic internal draft; feature not asserted to be deployed. Version: 1, 2026-09-28.

## Proposed feature

Test Match would rank relevant roles for a candidate and suggest visible candidate profiles to an employer recruiter using declared skills, work preferences, job criteria, and prior interactions only where a valid purpose and notice support that use. No automatic hiring, rejection, compensation, or background-screening decision is authorized by this record.

## Review before activation

The business owner must identify exact inputs, recipients, model/provider, training and retention policy, score meaning, override path, accessibility/accommodation route, human involvement, and monitoring. Product and legal reviewers must assess whether any NYC workflow is an automated employment decision tool and whether a bias audit, notice, public summary, or other requirements apply. Legal reviewer must assess EEOC employment-discrimination and disability guidance in the actual workflow. Do not infer that “human in the loop” alone resolves applicability.

## Decision gate

Current decision: **no release approval recorded**. Required evidence: current source inventory; bounded data-flow diagram; vendor contract/data-use terms; validity and adverse-impact evaluation on representative data; accessibility/accommodation test; jurisdiction analysis; candidate and employer notice; complaint/appeal route; accountable owner and review date. A synthetic document or model answer cannot satisfy this gate.
