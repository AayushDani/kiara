# Test candidate terms · working draft

Status: synthetic, unpublished draft. Version: 1, 2026-09-28. No candidate has accepted these terms.

## Service and account

Test would provide a technology-job marketplace for candidates to search roles, maintain a profile, control visibility, and submit applications. A candidate must provide accurate information they are authorized to share and keep account access secure. The service cannot promise a job, interview, employer response, or accuracy of third-party job content.

## Candidate content and sharing

The candidate retains rights in their submitted profile and documents. The proposed service permission is limited to operating the account, showing the profile according to the candidate's visibility choice, delivering a candidate-requested application, providing matching suggestions, and maintaining security. Application submission may provide a copy to the chosen employer, which may process it under its own notice and legal duties. Whether Test may use candidate content to train models is **not authorized by this draft** and requires a separate, explicit decision and notice.

## Platform safeguards

Test may remove fraudulent job content, restrict abusive recruiter accounts, and suspend access after a documented review. Candidates can report suspicious jobs, request an accommodation in a Test-controlled process, and raise a data-rights request. The details of appeal, deletion, and escalation require an operating policy and named contacts.

## Open legal terms

Entity name/address, eligibility, governing law, dispute process, liability terms, consumer-specific notices, contact routes, effective date, accessibility, and version acceptance record are **TBD**. Counsel must review them before publication.
