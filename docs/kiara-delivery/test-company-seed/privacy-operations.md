# Test privacy operations · working playbook

Status: synthetic internal draft, not an approved retention schedule or compliance attestation. Version: 1, 2026-09-28.

## Candidate rights intake

Record the request, claimed jurisdiction, channel, requested right, scope, and receipt time. Verify identity using a proportionate method without requesting unnecessary sensitive information. Separate Test-controlled profile/search/message data from employer-controlled application copies. Check for competing retention duties or a documented hold. Assign an owner and deadline using a reviewed jurisdictional rule; do not invent a universal deadline.

## Work and evidence

For an approved deletion request, enumerate primary profile records, application copies, recruiter messages, exports, indexes, analytics, providers, and backups. Record each system's deletion or exception result and any remaining retention window. Recheck derived data and downstream delivery. For access or correction, verify the exact records and current version before response. Keep the person's data out of broadly visible test notes.

## Incident handling

Triage suspected unauthorized access, preserve evidence, contain access, identify affected data and recipients, escalate to security and counsel, assess notification duties by location and contract, and document decisions. This pack creates no incident and no notification authority.

## Unresolved controls

Named request and security owners, provider inventory, backup expiry, legal hold process, approved retention periods, response templates, audit trail, and escalation contacts are **TBD**. No external message or deletion should be executed from this draft alone.
